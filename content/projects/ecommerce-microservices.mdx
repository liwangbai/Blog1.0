A production-grade e-commerce platform demonstrating microservices architecture patterns.

## Services

- **Catalog Service**: Product search, filtering, and inventory management
- **Cart Service**: Shopping cart with Redis-backed sessions
- **Order Service**: Order processing with sagas for distributed transactions
- **Notification Service**: Async email and push notifications via Kafka

## Architecture Decisions

- **gRPC** for inter-service communication — faster and more type-safe than REST
- **Kafka** for async event-driven communication between services
- **Saga Pattern** for distributed transactions (compensating transactions on failure)
- **API Gateway** (Envoy) for external traffic routing and rate limiting

## Learnings

Microservices add significant operational complexity. For this scale, a modular monolith would have been simpler to deploy and debug. The distributed tracing and log aggregation infrastructure was more effort than the business logic itself.
